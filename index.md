## 🧭 What Is Ration?

Ration is a friendly little helper that lives in your Windows system tray (the area at the bottom-right of your screen, next to the clock). It watches how much you use AI coding tools like Claude Code and Codex, then gently reminds you when you've hit your daily quota. Think of it as a smart kitchen timer for your coding sessions—it doesn't stop you from working, but it helps you pace yourself so you don't burn out or exceed your limits.

Whether you're a freelancer tracking billable hours, a student managing study time, or a developer trying to avoid AI over-reliance, Ration puts a simple, visual counter right where you can see it—without getting in your way.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Live Usage Tracking** – Ration quietly monitors your AI coding activity in the background. It works with popular tools like Claude Code and Codex, so you don't need to do anything special to start tracking.
- **Custom Quota Alerts** – Set your own daily or weekly limits. When you're close to reaching your quota, Ration pops up a gentle notification. When you hit it, you'll see a clear warning.
- **At-a-Glance Tray Icon** – The tray icon changes color based on your usage: green means plenty left, yellow means halfway there, and red means you're nearly or completely out. No need to open any window—just glance at your screen.
- **Simple, Clean Interface** – A tiny window shows your current usage as a progress bar, along with the time remaining until your quota resets. No clutter, no confusing menus.
- **Runs Quietly** – Ration starts with Windows (if you want) and sits quietly in the tray. It uses very little memory and won't slow down your computer.
- **Works Offline** – Everything is tracked locally on your machine. Your usage data never leaves your computer, so your privacy is fully protected.

## 🛠️ Customizing Your Quota

To make Ration work best for you, open the settings by right-clicking the tray icon and selecting "Settings."

### Setting Your Daily Limit

In the "Quota" section, you'll see a number box where you can enter how many minutes (or hours, if you prefer) of AI coding time you want to allow per day. For example, if you want to limit yourself to 3 hours, type `180` (minutes) or `3` (hours), depending on the unit you choose.

### Choosing Which Tools to Track

Under "Tracking," you'll see a list of supported AI tools. Make sure the ones you use (like Claude Code or Codex) have a checkmark next to them. If you use a tool that isn't listed, you can add it manually by typing its name in the "Add custom tool" box.

### Notification Preferences

In the "Notifications" tab, you can choose:
- When to alert you (e.g., at 50%, 75%, and 100% of your quota).
- The sound to play (or turn sound off).
- Whether to show a popup message or just change the tray icon color.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Ration free?

Yes, Ration is completely free to use. There are no hidden costs, subscriptions, or premium tiers. It's an open-source project, which means the code is publicly available for anyone to review or contribute to.

### Does Ration send my data anywhere?

No. Ration tracks everything locally on your computer. Your usage data is stored in a small file on your hard drive and is never transmitted over the internet. Your privacy is a top priority.

### Can I pause tracking temporarily?

Absolutely. Right-click the tray icon and select "Pause Tracking." This stops the timer for the current session without resetting your daily quota. You can resume at any time by selecting "Resume Tracking."

### What happens if I exceed my quota?

Ration will show a red tray icon and display a notification saying you've exceeded your limit. It won't block or stop you from using AI tools—it simply informs you. You can choose to keep working (the timer keeps counting) or take a break.

### How do I uninstall Ration?

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find "Ration" in the list, click the three dots next to it, and select "Uninstall." Follow the prompts, and Ration will be removed from your computer.

### Will Ration work with other AI tools besides Claude Code and Codex?

Ration has built-in support for the most common AI coding tools. For others, you can add custom tracking by specifying the application name in the settings. If you're not sure whether your tool is supported, just try it—if Ration doesn't detect it, add it manually.
